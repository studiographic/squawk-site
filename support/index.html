Support

Something not working, or stuck on a level? Write to squawk@studiographic.ca. Tell us your device and the level number, and we'll get back to you.

How do I play?

Drag the domino tiles onto the board and tap one to turn it. Every plane needs open sky in front of it and a clear path all the way to the edge of the board: no other plane in the way, and never the tower. Some planes face a corner and fly on the diagonal. Paths shows every route, green for clear and red for blocked. How to play on the title screen goes over the rules again.

I'm stuck on a level

Every puzzle has exactly one answer. A Hint locks one correct tile in place. Hints cost coins, which you earn from stars; a level's first hint costs one coin and each one after it costs one more. Coins can't be bought.

How do stars and ranks work?

A win without hints earns three stars, with one hint two, and with more one. Your rank, from S++ down to D, compares your moves, turns and taps with the level's par. Stars unlock planes and skies in the Hangar, and three-starring every level in a section turns its plane gold.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. Leave a level part way and it's waiting when you come back. Progress isn't synced between devices, and deleting the app deletes it.

How do I start over?

Open Options and choose Reset progress. This clears your wins, stars, coins and unlocks, and can't be undone.

Sound, music and vibration

Set sound and music volume, and turn vibration on or off, in Options.

Privacy

The game collects nothing and works offline. Read the privacy policy.
