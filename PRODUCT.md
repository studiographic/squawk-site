## Users

People who hear about Squawk (friends, a link from studiographic.ca, a share) and are on their phone most of the time. They like calm logic puzzles (Sudoku, nonograms, tile puzzles) and want to see quickly what the game is and when they can get it.

## Product Purpose

Squawk is a calm logic puzzle for iPhone and Android, made in Godot by Studiographic. You're the air traffic controller: fit domino tiles onto the board so every plane has open sky ahead and a clear path to the edge of the board, with no other plane or tower in the way. Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

This site says what Squawk is, shows it, explains the rule in a few seconds, and tells people it's coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. Success: a visitor understands the game, wants it, and knows to come back or email to hear when it's out.

## Capabilities and Constraints (true facts only)

- 57 puzzles across six tiers of airspace: Approach (tutorials), Tower 4×4, Sector 4×5, Corridor 5×6, Center 6×6, Storm 6×7.
- Every puzzle has exactly one solution (verified by the generator's solver).
- Controls: drag tiles onto the board, tap to turn. Paths (free) shows every flight path, green clear, red blocked. Hints lock one correct tile and cost coins.
- Stars (up to 3 per level), coins, a boarding pass after each win with a rank, and a passport of visa stamps per tier.
- Hangar: unlockable planes and skies (day, night, sunset, aurora), earned by stars.
- No prices, ratings, reviews, download counts or press exist; none may be invented.
