Flight rules

Every plane needs a clear path to the edge of the board. Green paths are clear; red ones are blocked.

  1. Fill the board

    Drag every tile onto the board until it's full, and tap a tile to turn it. Drag one off the board to send it back. Locked tiles stay put.

  2. Open sky ahead

    Every plane needs open sky in the square right in front of it: a square with no plane or tower. So a plane can never point straight off the board.

  3. Keep every path clear

    A plane's path runs straight ahead all the way to the edge. Nothing may block it: no other plane, and never the tower.

  4. Corners count too

    Some planes point diagonally. Their path runs on the diagonal all the way to the edge, with the same rules.

Every puzzle has exactly one answer. Stuck? Paths shows every route, green for clear and red for blocked.
