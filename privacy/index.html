Privacy policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

Squawk is a puzzle game by Studiographic. The short version: the game collects nothing, and nothing leaves your device.

What the game collects

Nothing. Squawk has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

To keep your place, Squawk saves these on your device only:

  • your progress: levels won, best times, stars, ranks, coins and the planes and skies you've unlocked
  • a board you left part way through, so you can pick it up again
  • your settings: sound and music volume, flight paths, vibration
  • a log of each level attempt: how long it took and how many moves, turns and hints were used. The game never sends it anywhere.

Deleting the app deletes all of this. Options › Reset progress erases your progress and unlocks without deleting the app.

Children

Squawk is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, children included.

This website

squawk.studiographic.ca uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. Google may set cookies to do this and processes the data under its own privacy policy. The site has no sign-up and doesn't ask for any personal information. If you email us, we use your address only to reply.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
